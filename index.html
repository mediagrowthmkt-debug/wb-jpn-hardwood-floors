Good to Know

Questions we hear before every project

How much does hardwood floor refinishing cost?
Refinishing is priced by square footage, the condition of the floor, and the finish you choose. You get a clear, documented estimate up front with no surprises after we start.
Should I refinish my floors or replace them?
If your boards are solid and structurally sound, refinishing restores them for a fraction of replacement. If there is deep water damage or the wear layer is gone, replacement makes more sense. We assess on site and tell you honestly.
Can I stay in my house while the floors are being done?
In most cases yes. The rooms being worked on are off-limits until the finish cures, but we protect the rest of the home and tell you exactly when each area is ready.
Is the job going to be dusty?
We isolate your kitchen, cover appliances and furniture, sand with dust-contained equipment, and do a full clean-up before we hand the home back.
Do you work with builders and general contractors?
Yes. A large part of our work is with builders, GCs, and property managers who need a reliable flooring crew for new construction and recurring projects.
What areas do you serve?
We are based in Medford, MA and serve a 100-mile radius including Boston, Cambridge, Somerville, the North Shore, southern New Hampshire, and Cape Cod. We come to you.
