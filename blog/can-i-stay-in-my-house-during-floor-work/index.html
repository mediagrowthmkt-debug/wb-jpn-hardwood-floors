Can I Stay in My House While the Floors Are Being Done?

In most cases, yes. Here's how we plan the work so daily life keeps moving.

A lot of homeowners assume they'll have to move out while their floors are done. Usually, you don't. Here's how we make it work.

The rooms in progress are off-limits, the rest of the home isn't

While a room is being sanded or is curing, you'll need to stay off it. But we seal off the work area and protect the rest of the home, so you can keep using the spaces that aren't being touched.

We plan the sequence with you

If we're doing the whole house, we phase the work so you always have somewhere to walk, sleep, and cook. We tell you the order up front and when each area will be ready to walk on.

  • Know which rooms are off-limits and for how long
  • Plan around the kitchen and main walkways
  • Keep pets and kids off freshly finished areas
Good planning is why most people never have to leave their home during a floor project.

Every project is a little different. Tell us about your home and your schedule, and we'll map out a plan that keeps daily life moving. Get a free estimate.
