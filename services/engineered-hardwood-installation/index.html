Engineered Hardwood Installation in Medford & Greater Boston

Stable, beautiful engineered flooring, ideal for basements, condos, and radiant-heat installs.

Get a Free Estimate

Engineered hardwood gives you a real-wood surface over a stable plywood core, so it handles moisture and temperature swings better than solid wood. That makes it a smart choice for basements, condos, and homes with radiant heat, without giving up the look of genuine hardwood.

What engineered installation includes

  • Subfloor prep, moisture testing, and leveling
  • Glue-down, nail-down, or floating installs as the space requires
  • Real-wood engineered planks in your chosen species and finish
  • Clean transitions, trim, and baseboard work
  • Installs over concrete and radiant-heat systems
  • Full clean-up and walkthrough

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between engineered and solid hardwood?
Solid hardwood is one full plank you can refinish many times. Engineered wood is a real-wood top layer over a stable core, better for moisture and radiant heat. We'll recommend the right one.
Can engineered floors be refinished?
Many can be refinished at least once, depending on the thickness of the top wear layer. We check the product spec and let you know.
Is engineered good for basements?
Yes. Its stability handles the moisture and temperature of below-grade spaces far better than solid wood.
