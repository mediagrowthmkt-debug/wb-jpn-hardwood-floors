Frequently asked questions

Can you fix just a few damaged boards?
Yes. We replace individual boards and blend them into the surrounding floor and finish.
My floor squeaks, can you fix it?
Usually, yes. Squeaks often come from movement in the subfloor or fasteners, which we secure.
Repair or full refinish?
If damage is isolated, a repair is cheaper. If the whole floor is worn, refinishing may be the better value. We assess and tell you honestly.
