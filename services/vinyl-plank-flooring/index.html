Frequently asked questions

Is luxury vinyl really waterproof?
Quality LVP is waterproof on the surface and through the plank. Proper installation and subfloor prep keep water from getting underneath.
Can you install LVP over my existing floor?
Sometimes, if it's flat and sound. Often we remove the old flooring and level the subfloor first for the best result.
How long does LVP last?
With good installation and normal care, quality LVP lasts many years, even in high-traffic and wet areas.
