Floor Sanding & Staining in Medford & Greater Boston

Smooth, even sanding and the exact stain color you want, oil or water-based, matched to your space.

A great finish starts with great sanding. We sand your floor flat and even, remove the old finish and imperfections, and apply the stain color you choose so the grain comes through the way you want it. The result is a smooth, consistent floor ready for a durable topcoat.

What sanding & staining includes

  • Multi-stage sanding for a flat, smooth surface
  • Dust-contained equipment to keep your home clean
  • Stain samples so you approve the color before we commit
  • Even color application that lets the grain show
  • Oil or water-based sealing coats
  • Edge and corner detail work by hand

Frequently asked questions

Oil-based or water-based finish?
Water-based cures faster, stays clearer, and has lower odor. Oil-based adds warmth and depth over time. We recommend the right one for your wood and how the room is used.
Can you match my existing floors?
In most cases yes. We test stains against your current floor to get as close a match as possible.
Is the dust really controlled?
Yes. We use dust-contained sanding and seal off the rest of the home, then clean up fully.
